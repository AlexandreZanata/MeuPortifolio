# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent and criticise ideas, not people.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and publishing someone's private
  information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
  project space.
- Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break these rules and may block
  repeat offenders.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/AlexandreZanata/MeuPortifolio/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@AlexandreZanata](https://github.com/AlexandreZanata) directly. Reports
are handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including the enforcement guidelines, applies
and is available at the link above.
